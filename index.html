02 / About

Curious about how things work — and stubborn enough to make them work.

I’m Sam, a systems tinkerer interested in the point where software meets physical hardware. Much of my work revolves around Linux, unsupported devices, and understanding exactly what happens beneath the abstractions.

My current focus is mobile Linux bring-up: working through device trees, initramfs configurations, bootloader behaviour, firmware, kernel sources, and driver integration for the Samsung Galaxy A22 5G.

I use C, Python, Bash, Git, Docker, radare2, and mtkclient depending on the problem. I enjoy reverse engineering unfamiliar systems, building reliable tooling, and turning a difficult hardware problem into documented, repeatable steps.
